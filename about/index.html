Our Story
20+ Years of Trusted HVAC Service

Westland Furnace LLC has an excellent reputation in the Westland MI area and over 20 years of experience in HVAC. We are a full-service company that can help with all of your heating and cooling needs — furnace repair, AC repair, installation, humidifiers, and air filtration units.

We work with both residential and commercial heating and cooling in Westland MI, as well as the entire Metro Detroit area. We offer 24-hour emergency service and will get your heating and cooling problems fixed quickly and at a competitive price.

Most of our clients are either repeat customers from over the years or family and friends referred to us by our current customers. After continuously getting referrals over all these years it tells us we must be doing something right. Our goal is to be your HVAC company for life — providing great service at a great price every single time.

All of our technicians undergo a rigorous training and apprentice program before they are allowed to perform service at your home or business. In addition, our technicians are required to attend quarterly continued education on new and improved HVAC technologies.

Our Community
Proud to Serve Westland, Michigan

Westland is a city in Wayne County, Michigan, located just west of Detroit. Known for its beauty and community spirit, Westland is home to the famous Hines Park — a beloved Wayne County park with miles of trails, picnic areas, and natural scenery along the Middle Rouge River. Westland is a welcoming suburb with a strong sense of community and a proud local identity.

We are proud to serve the Westland community and have built a strong reputation here for HVAC repair and installation over the past 20+ years. Our technicians live and work in the area and care deeply about the people they serve. Whether you need furnace repair on a cold Michigan winter night or AC service on a hot summer afternoon, we are just a phone call away.

If you have a need for furnace repair, air conditioning repair, or any other HVAC service in Westland MI or the surrounding Metro Detroit area, we hope you will give us a call. We look forward to hearing from you.
