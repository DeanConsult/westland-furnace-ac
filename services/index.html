AC Maintenance

Regular Maintenance Extends the Life of Your System

Temperatures in Westland Michigan can reach extreme highs in the summer, and your AC works overtime to keep you comfortable. Routine maintenance is crucial to keeping it running efficiently and avoiding costly repairs.

Coils, coil fins, filters, and condensate drains are all areas that should be consistently cleaned and maintained. Studies show that routine maintenance can increase the lifespan of your AC by up to two times. Call us today.
