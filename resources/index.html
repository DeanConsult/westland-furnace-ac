Maintenance Tips
Keep Your HVAC System in Top Shape
1

Change Your Filter Regularly

A clogged filter is one of the most common causes of HVAC problems. Check it monthly and replace it every 1–3 months. A clean filter improves airflow, air quality, and efficiency.

2

Schedule Annual Tune-Ups

Have your furnace inspected every fall and your AC serviced every spring. Annual tune-ups catch small issues before they become expensive repairs and keep your system running at peak efficiency.

3

Clear the Area Around Your Units

Keep at least 2 feet of clearance around your outdoor AC unit and furnace. Remove leaves, debris, and clutter. Good airflow prevents overheating and keeps your system running safely.

4

Check Your Thermostat

If your system isn't keeping up, check your thermostat settings first. A programmable or smart thermostat can reduce energy use by 10–15% by automatically adjusting temperatures when you're away or sleeping.

5

Listen for Unusual Noises

Banging, rattling, squealing, or grinding noises are your system's way of telling you something is wrong. Don't ignore them — early diagnosis prevents minor issues from becoming major repairs.

6

Don't Ignore High Energy Bills

A sudden spike in your gas or electric bill often means your HVAC system is working harder than it should — possibly due to a failing component, dirty coils, or refrigerant loss. Call us to diagnose the issue.
