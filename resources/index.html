Maintenance Tips
Tips for Keeping Your HVAC System in Top Shape
1

Change Your Filter Regularly

Check your furnace or AC filter every month and replace it every 1–3 months. A clogged filter reduces airflow, strains your system, and drives up energy bills. It’s the single easiest thing you can do to extend equipment life.

2

Schedule Annual Tune-Ups

Have your furnace inspected every fall and your AC serviced every spring. Annual maintenance catches small issues before they become costly repairs and keeps your system running at peak efficiency.

3

Keep Units Clear of Debris

Maintain at least 2 feet of clearance around your outdoor AC unit. Remove leaves, grass clippings, and clutter regularly. Blocked airflow causes the system to work harder and can lead to compressor failure.

4

Use a Programmable Thermostat

A smart or programmable thermostat can reduce energy use by 10–15% by automatically adjusting temperatures when you’re asleep or away. It’s one of the best investments for long-term savings.

5

Don’t Ignore Unusual Noises

Banging, rattling, squealing, or grinding sounds are warning signs. Banging on startup often means dirty burners — a fire and carbon monoxide risk. Don’t wait. Call a technician at the first sign of trouble.

6

Watch Your Energy Bills

A sudden spike in your gas or electric bill often means your system is working harder than it should — possibly due to a failing part, dirty coils, or refrigerant loss. If your bill jumps without an obvious reason, get your system inspected.
